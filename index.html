Frequently Asked Questions.

What is Pull to Refresh?

A curated intelligence newsletter covering the business of attention.

Pull to Refresh curates the best reporting on the business of attention from some of the world’s sharpest journalists and publications. Each issue brings together the deals, acquisitions, money moves, stock swings, ownership shake-ups, distribution shifts and competitive pressures shaping profitability and long-term value across attention-driven industries.

Published every weekday at 09:00 GMT+1. Free to read.

How is each issue of Pull to Refresh put together?

Every issue follows one simple rule: fresh news first, useful context second.

The Big Board brings you new developments from a stated reporting window, selected from the industries covered in that issue. If the week does not deliver enough stories worth your time, From the Archives steps in with older reporting that helps explain how the market got here. No random digging, either. We move backward through clearly defined reporting windows.

The Wire wraps things up by tracking public companies connected to those industries and showing how investors are pricing attention, distribution, competition and future growth.

Why should you trust Pull to Refresh?

Every issue of Pull to Refresh is built on receipts. We use company filings, earnings calls, investor presentations, regulatory disclosures, industry reports and reporting from trusted publications around the world. No mystery sources, no unsupported hot takes. Just information you can check for yourself.

Why subscribe?

We help our readers, typically industry enthusiasts, professionals, founders, investors, executives, operators and many others, understand better the industries that depend on attention as one connected business system by providing a central reporting view of the business of attention without requiring them to monitor dozens of separate sources themselves.

Why does Pull to Refresh matter to audiences in developed markets?

In developed markets, the attention economy is getting crowded. Streaming platforms, publishers, gaming companies, social networks, advertisers and creator businesses are all fighting for the same eyeballs, dollars and distribution.

Pull to Refresh helps you make sense of the noise. Every weekday, we curate the stories showing where attention is moving, who is gaining ground and why it matters.

Why does Pull to Refresh matter to audiences in emerging markets?

In emerging markets, the attention economy is still being built. New platforms, payment systems, media habits and business models are taking shape, often under very different conditions from those in developed markets.

Pull to Refresh gives readers a weekday look at how attention-driven businesses around the world are growing, competing and making money. The goal is not to copy what works elsewhere, but to understand the playbook, spot what could travel and see where local markets may create something better.

What is The Wire?

The Wire is our market-intelligence section. Each edition tracks publicly listed companies connected to the attention-driven industries covered in that day’s issue, using stock moves as signals for how investors are pricing attention, distribution, competition and future growth.

Pull to Refresh is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Google, Google Finance, any third-party market-data provider or any company featured in The Wire.

Does The Wire focus on just the US stock market?

For now, yes. A large share of the world’s most visible publicly traded attention businesses are listed in the US, including companies across media, entertainment, gaming, advertising, AI, streaming, platforms, retail and digital commerce.

That gives The Wire one consistent market to follow and makes it easier to compare how investors are pricing growth, competition, distribution and future demand across the industries covered in each day’s issue.

Other markets may join the watchlist over time. For now, the priority is keeping The Wire focused, comparable and genuinely useful.

Is The Wire investment advice?

No. The Wire tracks what stocks are doing and explains what those moves may reveal about attention-driven industries. It does not recommend buying, selling or holding any security.

Markets are unpredictable, and a stock move is a signal, not an instruction. Always do your own research and consider speaking with a qualified financial adviser before making an investment decision.
